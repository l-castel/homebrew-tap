# Copland privacy policy

*Last updated 19 November 2026*

Copland doesn't collect any data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it sends nothing to the
developer or to any third party.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything Copland stores stays on your device:

- **Your server address and username**, in the app's settings.
- **Your password**, in the system Keychain.
- **Music you save for offline listening and cached cover art**, in the app's own storage.

Signing out removes the password and server details. Deleting the app removes everything else.

## What the app sends, and to whom

Copland connects only to the Navidrome server you sign in to. It sends that server what's needed to play your music:
your login, what you browse and search for, the songs you play (so the server can keep play counts, and scrobble them
if you've set that up on the server), and changes you make to playlists and favorites.

That server is run by you or whoever gave you your account, not by Copland's developer, and its own privacy practices
apply to what it receives.

## Children

Copland collects no data from anyone, children included.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
